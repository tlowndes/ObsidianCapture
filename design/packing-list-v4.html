What changes from v3

  • Five kits in a chip row: Pocket, EDC bag, Packing, Car, Go bag (same on Windows Hangar › Kits).
  • Packing links your bags instead of copying them: EDC bag and the House-to-house pouch are one line each that open up.
  • Car grouped by your Car layout spots (driver's door, glove box, boot…), with Check now + date.
  • Go bag = tomtoc sling list, also a check, power bank charge reminder.
  • Pocket filled from EDC Checklist; Backpack → EDC bag with your 8 items.
  • Fix: 99 - Meta/Kits has only Sync clash copies ("Pocket (# Name clash…).md"), so the app can't see Pocket.md/Backpack.md. Build makes the app read clash copies too; any file rename waits for your OK.
