How it works
  • 100% = your usual day, set in Settings › Tasks (default 9 actions). 50% and 30% scale it; Rest moves every movable task.
  • Extras go to the lightest days this week, never past a day that's already full. Short tasks (≤15 min) stay first, like the Low energy chip.
  • Never moved: ★ High, bills, tasks with a time slot, events, repeats due today.
  • Untick a row to keep it today. Nothing changes until Move.
  • Moving rewrites the 📅 date on the task's own line in the vault (Quick Task or its note). Undo puts the dates back.
  • Picking a level also sets the Deck energy chip (Rest/30% = Low, 50% = Med, 100% = High), so the two agree.
  • Windows: same one-week list and a ⚡ Reshuffle button on the Upcoming page; same sheet as a dialog.
