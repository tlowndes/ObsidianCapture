The best way to run a home server in 2026

The Verge · 14 October 2026 · 9 min read

A small, quiet box under the desk can now do everything a rack of gear did ten years ago, from media to backups to home automation.

The single biggest change is power draw: the best mini PCs we tested idle at under 6 watts, which costs less a year than a streaming subscription.

We tried six mini PCs and two NAS boxes over a month of daily use, measuring noise and how painful each one was to set up from scratch.
