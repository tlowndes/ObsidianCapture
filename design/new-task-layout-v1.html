Same order as Edit task: text, date, length, Priority, Repeat, Tag, Place, then Add task. The kind row at the top replaces the Kind chip: ○ ★ – open their sheets as they do now. Typing "fri 15m" still fills the chips. The page scrolls if the keyboard covers it, and "Just added" stays below.
Why on the page, not a sheet
A sheet would add a tap before every task. On the page, a quick task is still type and tap Add task, with the extra rows there when you want them.

The Quick Task tile, share sheet and widgets stay as they are.

★ Priority becomes the same as picking ⏫ High in the Priority row, so the ★ sheet just opens with High picked, as built in 1.42.
